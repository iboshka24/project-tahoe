# Project identity and branding

**Maintainer:** [iboshka24](https://github.com/iboshka24)  
**Canonical repository:** <https://github.com/iboshka24/project-tahoe>

This policy concerns the identity of **this Project Tahoe project** and its
distinctive first-party branding. It does not change the licenses of its code.
No registration of a trademark is asserted by this document, and it does not
create rights beyond those available under applicable law.

## Accurate references are welcome

You may identify, discuss, review, package, link to, or describe compatibility
with Project Tahoe using its name truthfully. You may state that a fork is
"based on Project Tahoe" and link to the original repository. Attribution does
not imply endorsement. Do not remove copyright or license notices required by
the applicable software license.

## Make the source of a fork clear

If you distribute a modified version or fork:

- Clearly identify its maintainer and state that it is an **unofficial fork**.
- Use your own distinct product name and branding for the modified distribution;
  use "based on Project Tahoe" to describe its origin.
- Do not call it an official Project Tahoe release or imply approval, partnership,
  authorship, or support by iboshka24 without permission.
- Do not use first-party logos or other distinctive project branding in a way
  that makes your distribution, website, or account appear to be the official one.
- Preserve required upstream notices and distinguish your changes from the
  original work as required by the applicable license.

These are project branding guidelines. The MIT license does not grant trademark
rights; Apache-2.0 section 6 addresses trademarks and permits customary references
to origin and reproduction of NOTICE content. This policy does not restrict
software permissions granted by those licenses or rights independently provided
by law. It does not prohibit copying, modifying, selling, or redistributing code
in compliance with its license.

## Scope and third-party names

This policy does **not** claim the word "Tahoe" in every context, Apple's
macOS Tahoe name, Apple's logos, or the names and marks of Arch Linux, MacShell,
Hyprland, Wine, Darling, or other third parties. Those belong to their respective
holders. Only branding owned by this project falls within this policy; existing
third-party licenses remain applicable.

The project is independent and does not claim endorsement by those third parties.

## Permission and impersonation reports

For a branding permission request or a report of misleading attribution, contact
the maintainer through an issue in the canonical repository. Include the relevant
URL and describe the requested use or misleading claim. Do not post private data.
